veloster_n.bin, lamp_base.png and lamp_glow.png in this folder are an adaptation of

  "2022 Hyundai Veloster N" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2022-hyundai-veloster-n-d85ea2322d814899ad5905210f2c0e5a)
  by Ddiaz Design (https://sketchfab.com/ddiaz-design)
  licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)

Changes: converted to metres, engine and under-bonnet parts removed, decimated except the paint (about 406k to 116k triangles),
base-colour textures baked into vertex colours, wheels and callipers split for steering, lamp triangles sorted into
lamp groups (scripts/car3d_model.py). Only the lamp texture atlas is kept as images.
